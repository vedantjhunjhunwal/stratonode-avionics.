## 2. Thermal

- Battery box conductance: 0.084 W/K at cruise vs 0.094 W/K at sea-level pressure (thin air removes convection; radiation remains).
- Battery heater: 6.31 W steady at cruise, 3.29 W on the cold ground. Rated 12.6 W (2x margin) = 16.4 ohm at 14.4 V; warms the cold-soaked pack from -30 C to +5 C in 54 min.

## 3. Brown-out and hold-up

Worst load step (all loads at peak, both heaters on): 2.07 A from the pack.

| Case | Cell R (mOhm) | Pack R (mOhm) | OCV (V) | Terminal (V) | Margin to BMS cut-off (V) | Margin to buck minimum (V) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| heated pack, end of flight | 63 | 50 | 13.88 | 13.78 | 2.58 | 8.18 |
| cold-soaked start, 50% SOC | 217 | 174 | 14.72 | 14.36 | 3.16 | 8.76 |
| heater failed at cruise, 15% SOC | 332 | 265 | 13.88 | 13.33 | 2.13 | 7.73 |

Load at the moment of dropout, heaters included: 27.3 W. Riding through a 50 ms input dropout (connector fretting under vibration) from 12 V down to the buck's 5.6 V minimum needs **24251 uF** if every load stays on, but only **999 uF** when firmware sheds the radio, payload and both heaters within 0.35 ms (INA226 ALERT -> EXTI interrupt). Fitted: 3 x 470 uF 25 V aluminium polymer (1340 uF at -55 C, 34% margin). 
At -55 C the step caused by capacitor ESR is 27 mV with polymer parts vs 1821 mV with standard electrolytics, which is why the bulk capacitors are polymer.
